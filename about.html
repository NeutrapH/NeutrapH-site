Meet the Founder

A local idea, preparing to launch

I grew up in Welverdiend, Bushbuckridge. The taste and appearance of local borehole water helped inspire the idea for NeutrapH. These observations are not a laboratory assessment of water safety.

My background in Chemical Engineering and process engineering informs the proposed treatment approach. NeutrapH is still preparing to launch, with product details, quality verification and service arrangements to be confirmed before trading.

The aim is to develop practical water services around the needs of households, schools, churches and businesses in our community.

Chemical Engineering Process Engineer Born and raised in Bushbuckridge
Who We Are

Planning around community needs

NeutrapH is a pre-launch water business based in Welverdiend, Bushbuckridge. We are exploring purification, packaged water, refills, dispenser options and monthly packages.

We have not started trading. No opening date, delivery schedule or walk-in service is confirmed. Our name expresses our brand vision; it is not a claim about measured pH or certified water quality.

Our intended community

Who we hope to serve

The proposed services are intended for households and organisations in Bushbuckridge and surrounding communities. These are intended audiences, not existing customers.

Households

Households looking for convenient drinking-water options.

Schools

Learners and staff who need consistent, safe water throughout the school day.

Churches

Congregations and community gatherings that need reliable water supply for events and services.

Offices and Businesses

Businesses that need a professional, maintained water dispenser solution.

Football Clubs

Teams and sports clubs that need clean hydration for training sessions and match days.
