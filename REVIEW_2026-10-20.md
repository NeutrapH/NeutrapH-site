# Website consistency review — 20 October 2026

## Outcome

No blocking issue was found in the local checks described below. The current changes remain uncommitted. This is a review of the local pre-launch website, not a production deployment or certification of the proposed water treatment system.

## Scope

Reviewed the eight main pages: Home, Purification, Dispensers, Subscriptions, Refilling Station, Shop, About Us and Contact. Included the four supporting pages: privacy, launch information and both payment-return pages.

Checked shared navigation and footer, active navigation state, heading structure, page metadata, local links and image references, pricing qualifications, enquiry wording, responsive layout and selected interactive controls.

## Verification results

- All 12 existing automated checks passed.
- Pre-launch build validation passed without rewriting content.
- Git whitespace check passed.
- All 12 pages were checked in the browser at 390px and 1440px widths: one main heading per page and no page-wide horizontal overflow.
- The eight main pages were also checked at 768px: no page-wide horizontal overflow and no images missing an `alt` attribute.
- Loaded images in the mobile/desktop audit showed no failed image loads. This check does not assert that every offscreen lazy image was downloaded.
- Main-page active navigation matched the current page.
- Mobile navigation opened and closed with Escape.
- Contact opened with the Standard plan correctly selected from its URL parameter.
- Treatment animation paused successfully; selecting the RO membrane updated its description; Restart returned selection to the borehole stage.
- Homepage desktop appearance and mobile contact-form spacing were visually inspected.
- Shared navigation/contact information, local resource paths, responsive-image references, unique descriptions and production canonical URLs passed the automated checks.
- The Home enquiry form remains removed; the Contact form retains its labelled fields.
- Placeholder pricing and pre-launch wording remain consistent. Payment-return pages do not falsely confirm payment and remain excluded from indexing.

## Deliberate differences

- The purification hero uses a square industrial image to preserve the equipment composition; other heroes use landscape imagery.
- The process diagram has its own horizontal scrolling area on small screens so equipment and labels remain legible.
- The interactive diagram has eight selectable items; the explanatory cards combine pressure and membrane treatment and omit the final output as a separate treatment stage.
- Dark and light hero variants share the brand palette, typography and action styling.

## Remaining limits and future work

- No enquiries, emails, WhatsApp messages or payments were sent during this review.
- External map availability depends on Google and network conditions. An area-map link remains available alongside the embedded map.
- Production hosting, external social-account ownership, email delivery and real-device/cross-browser performance were not independently verified.
- Generated equipment imagery remains illustrative. Supplier models, final prices, availability, service terms and treatment performance still need business confirmation before launch.
- This was not a complete accessibility certification or security penetration test.
- Earlier industrial hero alternatives remain in the assets folder; they are not used by the current square hero and can be archived in a later asset-cleanup pass.
