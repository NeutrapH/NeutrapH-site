RO Process

The proposed treatment approach

This page explains the intended roles of treatment stages. It is not a commissioning record, laboratory report or certification of NeutrapH water.

  • Sediment filtration targets suspended particles
  • Carbon filtration targets selected substances affecting taste and odour
  • Reverse osmosis targets dissolved salts
  • UV is a proposed disinfection stage; performance requires validation

RO Process Flow Diagram

Raw Water Inlet

Sediment Filter

Intended to retain suspended particles.

Activated Carbon Filter

Intended to reduce selected substances; effectiveness depends on the media and operating conditions.

4

Carbon Block Filter

A proposed pre-treatment stage; final media and configuration need confirmation.

5

High Pressure Pump

Increases pressure so water can pass through the RO membrane.

6

RO Membrane

Intended to reduce dissolved salts; actual performance requires testing.

7

Clean Water Tank

Proposed storage stage; storage hygiene and controls need confirmation.

8

UV Sterilizer

Proposed UV treatment; performance depends on validated operating conditions.

9

Waste Water Reject

Concentrated impurities are flushed out of the system.
